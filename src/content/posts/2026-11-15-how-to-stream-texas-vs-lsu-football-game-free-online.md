College Football Playoff hopes are on the line tonight when the No. 6 Texas Longhorns take on the No. 21 LSU Tigers at Death Valley in Baton Rouge. If you're looking to catch this critical SEC matchup without cable, you're in luck. Several streaming services offer ways to watch the game live, and some even provide free trials that could score you a free viewing.

## The Stakes Are High for Both Teams

Texas arrives at 7-2 with three consecutive wins, including a dominant 34-20 victory over No. 22 Missouri last weekend. LSU, sitting at 6-3, is looking to bounce back after a brutal 31-13 home loss to Alabama. This matchup carries major playoff implications for both programs, and it marks the first time they'll face each other as conference mates. Their last meeting was back in 2019, when Joe Burrow-led LSU edged Texas 45-38.

The game kicks off Saturday, November 14 at 7:30 p.m. ET and 4:30 p.m. PT on ABC nationally.

## Best Streaming Options for Cord-Cutters

If you don't have a cable subscription, multiple live TV streaming services carry ABC and can get you in front of the action. Here's what you need to know about your best bets.

**DirecTV Stream** is a solid choice with ABC included in most of its packages, from the smaller MyNews and MySports Genre Packs all the way up to its larger Streaming TV offerings. Every DirecTV plan comes with a five-day free trial, which gives you a window to stream the Texas vs. LSU game without paying a dime. After your trial expires, plans start at $39.99 monthly.

**Fubo** is another excellent all-around live TV streamer that includes ABC in its Core and Ultra packages. Like DirecTV, Fubo sweetens the deal with a five-day free trial before plans renew at a discounted rate of $63.99 per month.

**YouTube TV** carries ABC across both of its live TV streaming plans, and you'll snag a free trial ranging from five to 21 days to get started. Once that trial ends, the more affordable plan renews at $49.99 monthly.

## Budget-Friendly Paid Option

If you want to skip the free trial and just subscribe directly, **ESPN Unlimited** is the most affordable route. This service delivers all ESPN and ESPN on ABC content in one place for just $31.99 a month. The catch? You won't get a free trial like the other services offer, but the lower price point makes it attractive for budget-conscious fans.
