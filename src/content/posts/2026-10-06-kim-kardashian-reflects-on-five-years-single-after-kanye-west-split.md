Kim Kardashian has become surprisingly candid about the longest stretch of her adult life spent without a romantic partner. In a recent interview with the U.K.'s Sunday Times, the 45-year-old reality TV star revealed that she spent five years single following her divorce from Kanye West and her brief fling with Pete Davidson, and she's got no regrets about it.

"I was really single for five years," Kardashian told the publication on October 3. "And that felt great. I could focus on my kids and projects that I really wanted to be involved with." It's refreshing to hear one of the world's most famous celebrities prioritize personal growth and family over the pressure to couple up.

## Choosing Healing Over Romance

In an industry where relationship status is often weaponized as tabloid fodder, Kardashian's deliberate decision to step back from dating deserves recognition. She emphasized feeling "no rush" to dive back into the dating world, noting that she felt emotionally complete during her solo years.

"Like, I really didn't feel empty," she continued. "I felt like I needed my time. I wanted to completely heal and just work on myself. I wasn't ready to open up. I really wasn't even thinking about it." This statement contradicts the persistent narrative that women, especially in the spotlight, must constantly pursue romantic validation.

Kardashian's timeline adds important context to her evolution. She filed for divorce from West in February 2021, dated Davidson from October 2021 to August 2022, and then apparently took significant time for herself. Their divorce was finalized in November 2022, meaning she genuinely did invest years in solo existence before her current romance. The couple shares four children together, making her focus on parenting during this period particularly understandable.

## A New Chapter

Though Kardashian declined to discuss her current relationship with British racing driver Lewis Hamilton, 41, the couple made their romance public in June after years of friendship. When asked about him directly, she replied with diplomatic restraint: "In the most respectful way, I'd probably rather not." Fair enough. Not every relationship needs to be dissected in the press.

Hamilton represents a notably different chapter for Kardashian, one that emerged after she had supposedly done the internal work necessary for a healthier dynamic. Her willingness to wait for the right situation rather than settle speaks volumes about her maturity.

## The Pete Davidson Perspective

Interestingly, Pete Davidson himself recently validated Kardashian's narrative from a different angle. The comedian told Variety that the bullying he endured from Ye during their brief relationship actually "cleared the path" for her to date again. West's relentless public trolling through violent lyrics, social media attacks, and derogatory nicknames was well documented and deeply inappropriate.

"I like to think I got that one out of the way for her because whoever that guy was going to be was gonna get it," Davidson said. While his framing is somewhat self-aggrandizing, it does highlight how toxic external pressure can impact someone's capacity to trust and connect.

Davidson also offered genuine praise for Kardashian and her family, comparing dating a Kardashian woman to surviving boot camp. He found their transparency and comfort with their own lifestyle "very attractive and mature." It's a testament to how Kardashian has maintained dignity throughout complicated [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) situations.

## Why This Matters

Kardashian's narrative about choosing five years of solitude is particularly valuable in our current culture of constant connection and dating app fatigue. She modeled something radical: the idea that your worth isn't tied to your relationship status, and that sometimes the best investment you can make is in yourself.

Her openness about healing after a high-profile divorce and public heartbreak normalizes the messy, non-linear nature of moving forward. Not everyone needs to immediately rebound. Not everyone should.

The irony is that by stepping away from the dating scene entirely, Kardashian seems to have found something worth stepping back into. Sometimes the universe does work in your favor when you stop frantically trying to orchestrate every outcome.
