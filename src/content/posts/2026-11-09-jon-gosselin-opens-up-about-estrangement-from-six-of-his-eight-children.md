Jon Gosselin is speaking candidly about one of the most painful aspects of his life: his estrangement from six of his eight children. In a recent appearance on the "Legally Goff" podcast, the former "Jon & Kate Plus 8" star opened up about relationships that have deteriorated to the point of near-complete disconnect.

The reality TV personality shares eight children with his ex-wife Kate, whom he split from in 2009. His kids include 26-year-old twins Mady and Cara, and 22-year-old sextuplets Collin, Hanna, Aaden, Alexis, Joel, and Leah. Despite these biological ties, Jon revealed that his connection to most of them has all but vanished.

## A Father's Painful Admission

"I haven't seen Mady and Cara in like forever," Jon said during the podcast interview. "I have pictures of them, but I still wouldn't be able to pick them out on the street. That's how much I haven't seen them." The comment cuts deep, illustrating just how complete the separation has become. He hasn't attended their graduations or proms, events that typically mark important milestones in a parent's relationship with their children.

Jon last saw the twins when they were approximately 13 years old. Now they're adults, and he admits knowing virtually nothing about their lives. "I don't know anything about my twins, if they have boyfriends or girlfriends, or what favorite color they have now, if they drive a car. I know nothing," he said.

The former [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) figure describes the situation as "alienation" and characterizes it as a form of silence that transformed him overnight from an engaged, active parent into someone completely removed from his children's lives. He places significant blame on TLC, the network that aired his family's show, suggesting that his kids have adopted a negative narrative about him.

## The Blame Game

According to Jon, Kate allegedly told their son Collin a specific narrative about his father that ultimately shaped how Mady and Cara view him. "They believe the narrative of TLC that I'm a shitbag and that I don't want to see them," Jon stated. "That's exactly what she said to Mady and Cara and that's their mother and they believed her and that was it."

Not all of Jon's relationships with his children remain fractured. He does maintain contact with Collin and Hannah among the sextuplets. However, he last saw Joel, Aaden, Alexis, and Leah back in 2018, making it over five years since that contact.

## The Backdrop of Alleged Abuse

Collin's situation adds another layer of complexity to this family saga. The 22-year-old has alleged that he experienced physical and emotional abuse at the hands of his mother, including being confined to a locked basement room with cameras. These allegations, made public in 2024, paint a troubling picture of family dysfunction that extends beyond simple estrangement.

Kate has not directly addressed all of Collin's claims but responded to commenters on social media, stating: "I know who I am and my kids know who I truly am and to me, that's all that matters." Her response suggests she believes her actions speak for themselves, though the allegations remain serious and unresolved in the court of public opinion.

## What This Means

Jon's openness about his estrangement raises difficult questions about parental rights, media influence, and family reconciliation in the age of [reality television](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment). Whether one believes Jon's account or Kate's implied version, the result is undeniably tragic: eight adult and near-adult children with a fractured relationship to their father and unresolved family trauma.

The "Jon & Kate Plus 8" phenomenon once captivated millions of viewers who watched this family grow up on screen. Now, years later, the legacy appears to be one of broken relationships and lingering pain rather than cherished memories.
