The holiday season is officially kicking off on NBC tonight, and it's coming with some serious star power. Ariana Grande, Robert De Niro, and Ben Stiller are teaming up for a special appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon this Sunday, November 8, bringing the festive vibes early to the late-night crowd.

## A Christmas Caroling Moment You Won't Want to Miss

The three stars are joining Jimmy Fallon for a surprise performance of the classic tune "O Christmas Tree" as part of the network's celebration of the 2026 Rockefeller Center Christmas tree arrival. It's the kind of wholesome, unexpected moment that makes late-night television genuinely fun to watch. Grande takes lead vocals, naturally delivering those impressive high notes that have made her a pop powerhouse. Stiller brings earnest charm to his part, while De Niro's involvement in the caroling trio adds an element of intrigue that you'll want to see for yourself.

The trio won't just sing and run. They'll be sitting down with Fallon for an extended chat throughout the episode, diving into stories and banter as only Hollywood veterans can. It's the kind of relaxed, conversational interview that fans genuinely enjoy, especially when multiple stars riff off each other.

## Promoting the Latest Meet the Parents Chapter

This appearance is part of the promotional blitz for "Focker-in-Law," the latest installment in the beloved Meet the Parents franchise hitting theaters on November 25. Grande joins the ensemble as Olivia Jones, who's dating Henry Focker, the now-grown son of Greg and Pam Focker (played by Stiller and Teri Polo). The casting is interesting because it brings fresh energy to a franchise that's been dormant for a while, introducing Grande to audiences in a comedic, family-friendly context.

For Grande, this represents another step in her evolution as an entertainer. While she's primarily known as a [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) superstar, these kinds of acting gigs showcase her range beyond music. For Stiller and De Niro, it's a reminder that the Focker franchise still has cultural relevance and can draw A-list talent.

## Tonight Show's Special Post-Football Format

The November 8 episode is the second in a series of four special Tonight Show episodes airing immediately after NBC's Sunday Night Football broadcasts this season. The first special aired on September 27 with Sylvester Stallone and Luke Bryan. This format is smart programming, capturing viewers already tuned to NBC for football and keeping them engaged with premium late-night content.

Lil Baby will also appear as the musical guest on tonight's episode, adding another layer of entertainment value. The episode begins at 11:35 p.m. ET following the Buccaneers vs. Bears game.

## What's Next on the Tonight Show Schedule

The special post-football episodes continue with Jennifer Lopez and Anthony Mackie appearing on November 15, alongside a musical performance from Quavo. Another special episode is scheduled for January 10, with guests yet to be announced. This strategy of clustering major celebrity appearances around sports broadcasts shows NBC's commitment to making late-night television an event viewing experience rather than just background noise.

For those invested in either [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) news or the resurgence of the Focker franchise, tonight's appearance is essential viewing. The combination of Grande's star power, De Niro's gravitas, and Stiller's comedy prowess creates an unmissable moment. Whether you're a longtime fan of the Meet the Parents films or just curious about what happens when these three share a microphone, there's something here for everyone.
