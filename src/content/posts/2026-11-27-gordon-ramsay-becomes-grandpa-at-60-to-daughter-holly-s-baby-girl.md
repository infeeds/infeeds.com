## Welcome to Papa G's World

Gordon Ramsay is officially a grandfather, and he couldn't be happier about it. The fiery British chef, now 60 years old, got the best Thanksgiving gift imaginable when his daughter Holly Ramsay-Peaty welcomed her first child with Olympic swimmer Adam Peaty on November 25.

The couple announced the arrival of their baby girl, Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty, via Instagram on Thursday with the caption "Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty, Welcome to the World." Photos showed Holly and Adam cradling their newborn in the hospital, beaming with joy. Gordon wasted no time celebrating in the comments section, writing, "Welcome Miss Daphne, congratulations to Adam & Holly wow wow wow."

It's a moment Ramsay has been openly excited about for weeks. Just days before Daphne's arrival, the Hell's Kitchen star appeared on Jimmy Kimmel Live and gushed about his impending grandfather status. "To be a granddad, you know, in your 60s, I think is amazing," he told the late-night host enthusiastically. "We are so excited."

## From Baby Bump to Birth

Holly and Adam didn't keep their pregnancy secret for long. Back in June, the couple revealed their plans to expand their family by posting a photo of Holly's growing baby bump alongside the caption "Baby Ramsay-Peaty coming December 2026." They arrived a bit early for the holidays, but no one's complaining about welcoming Daphne just in time for Thanksgiving festivities.

The couple, who married in December 2025, seem thrilled with their new arrival. Holly made sure to thank all the medical professionals who helped bring Daphne into the world safely. "Thank you to all the Doctors, Nurses and Midwives who made sure we were safe, healthy and happy - we will forever be grateful," she wrote in her announcement post.

What makes this moment particularly special is that Daphne isn't just joining her parents' lives, but also becoming a big sister. Holly mentioned in her announcement that Daphne will be "George's baby sister," indicating the couple already has another child.

## A Grandpa With Style

Gordon Ramsay has never been one to do anything quietly or without flair. At his Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony, held just days before Daphne's birth, the chef revealed exactly what his new granddaughter will call him. "I think I'm going to be called Papa G. Papa G. with a star in Hollywood!" he proudly told People magazine and other publications.

It's a name that perfectly captures Ramsay's larger-than-life personality. The celebrity chef is known for his passionate approach to cooking, his brutally honest critiques on [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) television shows, and his surprising warmth when it comes to family matters. Becoming Papa G represents another chapter in his very public life.

## The Big Family Picture

Holly is Gordon Ramsay's second eldest child. The 60-year-old shares six children total with his wife Tana Ramsay, meaning he has plenty of experience in the parenting department. While most of his kids are grown, he's also still actively raising his two youngest sons, Oscar, 7, and Jesse, 3.

During his Jimmy Kimmel appearance, Ramsay reflected on juggling being both a father to young children and now a grandfather. "It's nice that we've still got young boys as well, at the same time," he explained to the host. "So, yeah, I'm looking forward to it."

This multi-generational household dynamic speaks volumes about where Ramsay is in his life at 60. He's not slowing down but rather expanding his empire in every way, from his restaurants to his television ventures to his growing family tree.

Becoming Papa G to Daphne marks a significant milestone for the globally recognized [chef](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment), one that shows even the most famously hot-tempered personalities in culinary television have soft spots for family moments.
