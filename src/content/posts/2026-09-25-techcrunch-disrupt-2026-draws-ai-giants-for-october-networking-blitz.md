TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 is shaping up to be the year's biggest [tech](https://infeeds.com/tags/tech) gathering, with heavy hitters like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Replit commanding six industry stages at San Francisco's Moscone West on October 13-15. If you've been on the sidelines during the current industry shifts, this is your moment to reconnect with the ecosystem.

The event is pulling out all the stops to make attendance accessible. Early bird pricing offers savings up to $200 plus 50% off a second ticket, valid through September 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT. But there's something even more noteworthy: if you've recently been laid off, TechCrunch is offering 100 Expo+ Passes for just $75. These discounted passes are available on a first-come, first-served basis through October 13, when the conference kicks off.

## Why Disrupt Matters for Your Next Move

Layoffs have become a fixture of the tech industry landscape. What makes Disrupt valuable isn't just job hunting, though the expo hall will host 300+ startups and countless hiring conversations. It's the concentrated networking opportunity. Three full days with 10,000+ founders, investors, operators, and tech leaders creates a density of meaningful connections you simply won't find on LinkedIn or traditional job boards.

The conference floor itself is where the magic happens. The Expo Hall serves as the central hub where all attendees converge, creating organic opportunities to learn about companies before you apply, meet founders directly, and stumble into conversations that lead somewhere unexpected. These chance encounters often matter more than scheduled interviews.

## Networking Before You Arrive

Disrupt understands that relationship-building doesn't start on day one. When you purchase your ticket, you gain access to the TechCrunch Events app, which uses AI-powered matchmaking to help you discover relevant people based on your interests and goals. This means you can start building your network immediately, then take those digital connections offline through 1:1 meetings and Expo Hall conversations once the conference begins.

For those exploring [careers](https://infeeds.com/tags/careers) outside traditional employment, Disrupt offers breakout sessions where tech leaders share practical insights. You'll hear directly from people shaping the startup landscape, get answers to your pressing questions, and gather intelligence that informs your next moves, whether that's a job search, interview preparation, or pivoting into a new sector entirely.

## What's on Stage

The breadth of companies headlining this year's stages reflects where the industry is focused: artificial intelligence and infrastructure. OpenAI and Anthropic's prominent placement signals that generative AI isn't just a trend anymore, it's foundational to how tech companies are being built. Replit's presence highlights the developer tools renaissance, where building and deploying software is becoming more accessible to smaller teams.

Having six dedicated industry stages means there's serious programming beyond the expo floor. This is where you'll hear about emerging trends, see product demonstrations, and get tactical advice from operators who've navigated similar challenges.

## The Layoff Pass Is Real Support

The $75 Expo+ Pass for recently laid-off workers isn't performative corporate gesture. It's designed to remove financial barriers to networking when it matters most. Yes, 100 passes is a limited number, but it acknowledges that the people most likely to benefit from Disrupt are often those with tighter budgets immediately after job loss.

The pass doesn't include access to industry stage sessions and roundtable discussions, so if you need the full experience, other ticket tiers are available. But for pure networking and exploration of what's happening in the [startup](https://infeeds.com/tags/startup) ecosystem, the Expo+ Pass gets you where you need to be.

The deadline for early bird pricing is September 25. The Layoff Pass window closes October 13 at 8 a.m. PT or when all 100 passes are claimed, whichever comes first. If you've been sidelined by recent industry volatility, putting yourself back in the room where opportunities are taking shape could be exactly what you need right now.
