Rolling Loud is making a triumphant return to San Bernardino next December, with the three-day hip-hop festival set to take over Nos Events Center from December 10-12, 2027. The move marks the eighth time the festival lands in Southern California and represents a strategic shift away from the Hollywood Park venue in Inglewood, which hosted the California edition from 2023 to 2025.

Co-founder and CEO Matt Zingler explained the reasoning behind the relocation in an interview with Billboard. "San Bernardino has always been a great location to do the shows for us. There's a ton of fans out there," he said. The venue, previously hosting Rolling Loud in 2017 and 2021, offers advantages over major urban centers. "Sometimes, when you're in major cities, it's more expensive for fans and also more congested."

## Building the Festival Experience

Zingler emphasized that the Nos Events Center provides better infrastructure for experimentation and creativity. "We can experiment more with the site and build more activations. All in all, it's better grounds for festivals than some of the L.A. proper spaces," he explained. This philosophy reflects Rolling Loud's broader approach to [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) and fan engagement.

The festival franchise has grown exponentially since its launch in 2015. Beyond California, Rolling Loud has established itself in Miami, Northern California, New York, Portugal, Australia, Canada, the Netherlands, Germany, Thailand, Austria and India. The international expansion has occasionally hit bumps, most notably when organizers canceled the 2026 Australian dates in March due to delays with local partners.

Zingler's confidence in Rolling Loud's market position is palpable. When asked about competition, he was direct: "I don't really know of any other festivals booking urban music right now. We don't have competition. We're the biggest hip-hop festival brand." This assertion reflects not just market dominance but also a commitment to staying relevant. He noted that the festival continuously adjusts its approach to maintain cultural currency.

## Growing Global Presence

The brand's international footprint continues expanding strategically. Rolling Loud India welcomed 65,000 fans over two days in 2025 and will return to Mumbai from December 12-13, 2026. Zingler expects the second edition to significantly exceed the inaugural event. "The first year in Mumbai was training the fans because it was very local crowd-wise, so a lot of people in India had never experienced a hip-hop show before," he explained. "Now that they've experienced Rolling Loud, they know what to expect. I think we will definitely hit a higher capacity."

The localization strategy extends to artist selection and experience design. "When we go internationally, we switch it up a lot. Every place that we go, they digest music differently," Zingler said. This approach ensures that Rolling Loud feels authentic to regional audiences rather than presenting a cookie-cutter experience globally.

Meanwhile, the Miami edition continues thriving. Rolling Loud took over Camping World Stadium in Orlando this May and will return to Florida for its traditional slot on May 7-9, 2027. The festival has weathered significant challenges over the past decade, from pandemic disruptions to economic uncertainty and industry-wide cancellations.

## Beyond the Festival Grounds

Rolling Loud's expansion reaches beyond live events. The franchise is receiving the Hollywood treatment with a feature film starring Owen Wilson, Matt Rife, Christine Ko and Henry Winkler, releasing October 2. Partially filmed during Travis Scott's 2024 Miami appearance, the comedy follows a father searching for his teenage son in the festival crowd, inspired by director Jeremy Garelick's personal experience. Several Rolling Loud performers including Scott, Sexyy Red, Ski Mask The Slump God and Ty Dolla $ign make appearances in the film.

Zingler's philosophy on brand longevity is worth noting: "If you don't grow with your fans and keep it new, your brand withers and dies. But if you're constantly adjusting and have your finger on the pulse, you can go forever."

Passes for the 2027 California festival go on sale September 24 at 11 a.m. PT, with general pre-sales beginning at 12 p.m. PT. The lineup announcement will come later.
