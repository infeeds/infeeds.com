## A Grudge That Never Faded

Christina Ricci recently opened up about a lingering feud with Cher that has lasted decades, tracing its roots back to a simple mistake made when she was just 19 years old. During an appearance on Andy Cohen's "Watch What Happens Live," the "Wednesday" actor revealed that the iconic singer still hasn't forgiven her for failing to return a voicemail.

The two starred together in the 1990 film "Mermaids," where Cher played the mother to both Ricci and Winona Ryder. Despite working closely on set, their professional relationship took an unexpected turn after an unflattering interview went public.

## How It All Started

Ricci explained that when she was a young actor, she participated in what she believed was a Rolling Stone interview (though she acknowledged it might have been Première magazine). During that interview, Cher apparently made some critical comments about the rising star.

"She said all these things that were not that amazing about me," Ricci told Cohen, recounting the incident with candor.

But here's where the story gets interesting. Cher didn't let the situation fester. The singer immediately tried to make amends by leaving Ricci a voicemail explaining the circumstances behind her comments.

"She left me a voicemail about how journalists are always trying to get the worst things, and blah blah blah," Ricci said. "And she was so sorry."

## The Mistake That Mattered

At 19, Ricci was young and perhaps not yet equipped to understand the significance of Cher's attempt at reconciliation. She never returned the call.

"I didn't return that message," Ricci admitted to Cohen.

Since that fateful moment, Ricci has attempted to rebuild the bridge. "I've reached out to her since and gotten radio silence," she revealed, noting the painful reality of being on the receiving end of Cher's silent treatment.

What makes this story particularly poignant is Ricci's genuine admiration for [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) legend Cher. "I adore her. She's the best," Ricci said during her appearance. She also demonstrated remarkable self-awareness about the situation.

## Understanding Cher's Perspective

Ricci offered a compelling analysis of how Cher must feel looking back at the interaction. She walked through the scenario from the icon's viewpoint: "I absolutely 100% can imagine being Cher and being like, 'Oh my god, they 100% misquoted me. I'm going to call this young person and explain to her that they misquoted me.' And then the gall of that young person to not return the call and be like, 'Oh my god, Cher, it's OK. I love you so much.' I would be so pissed. I get it."

This perspective reveals a maturity that likely comes with age and experience in Hollywood. Ricci understands that Cher's grudge isn't unreasonable, even as she wishes things could be different between them.

The situation raises broader questions about [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) industry relationships, generational gaps in communication, and the weight that celebrities carry when they reach out personally to others. Cher took the time to explain herself to a young actor during the height of her fame, only to be ignored.

HuffPost reached out to representatives for Cher for further comment, though no statement has been forthcoming.

This story serves as a reminder that in our increasingly fast-paced world, a simple voicemail returned could have changed the trajectory of two accomplished artists' relationship.
