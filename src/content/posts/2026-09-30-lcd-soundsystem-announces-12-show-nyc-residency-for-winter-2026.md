LCD Soundsystem is doubling down on their beloved tradition of ending the year in New York City. The iconic Brooklyn band just announced a massive 12-show residency spanning three weekends in late 2026, continuing a nearly decade-long custom of giving fans multiple chances to catch them live during the holiday season.

The residency kicks off November 19-22 at Knockdown Center in Queens, followed by a return to Brooklyn Steel for four nights from November 30 through December 3. They'll wrap things up with another four-night stand at Knockdown Center from December 10-13. It's the kind of extended commitment to their hometown that you rarely see from major acts anymore.

## A Historic Milestone

Here's what makes this particularly special: the November 30 show at Brooklyn Steel marks LCD Soundsystem's 100th residency performance. To commemorate the occasion, the band is donating 100 percent of ticket proceeds from that night to a charity they'll announce later. It's a meaningful gesture that underscores just how important these residencies have become to the band's identity and their relationship with New York audiences.

In many ways, these year-end runs have become the closest thing LCD Soundsystem offers to consistent live performances. The group hasn't released a new album since 2017's "American Dream," and they haven't dropped original material since 2022. While that's left fans waiting quite a while for fresh studio work, these residencies have proven to be a reliable outlet for the band to connect with supporters and road-test new directions.

Interestingly, while LCD Soundsystem itself has been relatively quiet on the new music front, guitarist Al Doyle just released "Hard Times in America," a track from his forthcoming solo debut "Hollywood Saviour." It's unclear whether we'll hear any of that material during the residency, but it does suggest the band members are actively creating.

## What to Expect

The 2026 residency will feature live openers at various shows, plus afterparties with DJ sets from special guests. Those details matter because LCD Soundsystem residencies have always been about more than just the main event. They're full experiences designed to reflect the energy and community of New York's [music scene](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment).

For fans eager to secure tickets, there are multiple on-sale opportunities. American Express Card Members get first crack starting Tuesday, October 6 at 10 a.m. ET through Thursday, October 8 at 10 p.m. ET. Fan presales run from Wednesday, October 7 at 10 a.m. ET through the same Thursday evening deadline. General public sales begin Friday, October 9 at 10 a.m. ET.

Given LCD Soundsystem's stature and New York's insatiable appetite for the band, expect these shows to sell quickly. The combination of three different venues, the milestone 100th show celebration, and the charitable component of one evening should make this residency more compelling than ever.

## Why This Matters

What's remarkable about LCD Soundsystem's commitment to these residencies is how they've reshaped what it means for a band to be "from" somewhere. In an era when touring often means parachuting into cities for single nights, LCD Soundsystem chose to plant themselves in New York for extended runs. It's become their signature move, and it's created a genuine tradition that fans look forward to annually.

These aren't album cycle tours or victory laps. They're sustained engagements that treat the band's hometown as something worth a real time investment. Whether LCD Soundsystem releases new material before these shows remains to be seen, but honestly, the residencies themselves have become the main event. That's a rare accomplishment in modern music.
