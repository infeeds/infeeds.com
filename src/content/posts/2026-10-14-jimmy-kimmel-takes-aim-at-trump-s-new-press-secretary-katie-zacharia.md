Jimmy Kimmel didn't hold back on Tuesday night when he set his sights on Katie Zacharia, the latest addition to Donald Trump's rotating cast of press secretaries. During his monologue on Jimmy Kimmel Live, the host delivered a blistering critique of Zacharia's performance during her inaugural briefing at the White House podium.

## A Rocky Debut

Kimmel began by referencing Trump's unusually long tenure in office, joking that the president has been in power for "about 50 or 60 years." To keep things fresh, Kimmel noted, the administration cycles through "a new fawning blonde dolt" every six months to maintain the president's interest.

Zacharia's first briefing lasted only 14 minutes, a duration Kimmel found both telling and amusing. "Even in that short time she accomplished exactly what she was brought in to do, which is slobber a whole tube of lip gloss all over the president's behind," Kimmel quipped with characteristic snark.

The late-night host played a clip of Zacharia expressing her long-held desire to work for Trump, which prompted Kimmel to deadpan: "All it takes is that and dressing like you're about to announce pre-boarding on a Delta Airlines flight." When footage showed Zacharia reading mechanically from prepared remarks, Kimmel seized the opportunity to deliver another jab: "Her reading is good for an 11 year old. He hired a fourth grader to be press secretary!"

## A Questionable Background

Zacharia's path to the White House podium raised eyebrows throughout the segment. Kimmel detailed her background as what he called a "real piece of work," noting that she gained prominence by "calling Democrats pedophiles on social media and giving weight to Pizzagate." Given this history, Kimmel sarcastically suggested that viewers should expect her to "immediately call for everyone named in the Trump-Epstein files to be punished to the full extent of the law."

The host then turned his attention to Zacharia's media appearances as a talking head, where she made bold predictions about film and culture. Most notably, he highlighted her claim that the "woke movie" The Odyssey would fail commercially. "Well, that woke movie went on to make $1.8 billion," Kimmel said with mock amazement. "So we got a smart one here."

## The Revolving Door Continues

This marks the sixth time Trump's administration has [changed press secretaries](https://infeeds.com/tags/politics), a pattern that has become almost routine. Zacharia replaces Karoline Leavitt after her recent departure, and before her position, she served as a junior spokesperson under former Secretary of Homeland Security Kristi Noem.

The rapid turnover at this particular position has become fodder for [late-night comedy](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) and serious political commentary alike. Kimmel concluded his segment by asking how long Zacharia will actually survive in the role, especially after such a public mocking on national television.

"Katie's first outing was, as her boss might say, a total disaster," Kimmel wrapped up. "I was impressed though: Do you know how hard it is to become Trump's worst press secretary that quickly?"

The segment highlighted what has become an increasingly frustrating aspect of political theater for observers across the spectrum. Whether one finds Kimmel's critique amusing or heavy-handed, the underlying question remains worth considering: what does the constant cycling of press secretaries say about the sustainability and stability of the current administration?
