If you've been eyeing Mustard Made's storage lockers but balked at the price tag, now's your moment. Through November 11, the company is slashing 25 percent off everything site-wide, including their signature flat-pack lockers that have won over the WIRED Reviews team.

Mustard Made has built a reputation on beautiful, functional storage that actually looks good in your home. Their lockers are available in 12 different colors, so whether you're going minimalist or bold, there's something to match your aesthetic. What makes them special isn't just the design, though. These are genuinely useful pieces that solve real storage problems.

## Why These Lockers Stand Out

The Lowdown is a personal favorite for anyone who needs entertainment storage with style. This locker is specifically designed with wire cutouts on the back panel, making it perfect for setting up a record player or media setup without the mess of visible cables. A record player can sit comfortably on top with speakers, while records, accessories, and other entertainment essentials store neatly inside. It's the kind of piece that draws compliments from visitors because it looks intentional and polished, not like a storage compromise.

WIRED senior Gear editor Julian Chokkattu is also a convert. He appreciates how the lockers snap shut securely and come with keys for locking up valuables. The range of sizes means you can find exactly what you need, whether that's a tall, skinny locker reminiscent of your high school gym or a short, squat version for more modest spaces. They're magnetic too, which opens up even more organizational possibilities.

## Assembly and Setup

Let's be honest: flat-pack furniture can be a gamble. But Mustard Made includes helpful video instructions online, which is a game-changer compared to wrestling with printed paper manuals. Yes, assembly takes some time, but it's straightforward enough that you won't need professional help. The pieces fit together well, and the quality feels solid once you're done.

There's also the matter of customer service. When hardware gets lost during a move, Mustard Made will send replacements for free. That kind of support, even if your experience varies, suggests a company that stands behind its products.

## Perfect for Renters and Flexible Living

These lockers are apartment-friendly, which matters more than ever as living situations become more temporary and flexible. They're not permanently installed, so renters can actually decorate without losing their security deposit. For anyone who moves frequently or likes to rearrange their space, that's huge. You get real storage and real style without commitment.

## The Catch

Inventory shifts as colors sell out, so if you're eyeing a specific shade, don't wait too long. The 25 percent discount applies to everything on their site, from the iconic lockers to cabinets, shelves, and other organizational products. That makes this a rare opportunity to expand your storage setup at a more reasonable price point.

Mustard Made lockers are investment pieces. They're not cheap, even at 25 percent off. But if you care about how your home looks and functions, they're worth considering for [storage](https://infeeds.com/tags/storage) solutions that won't make you cringe when guests come over. This sale just makes a premium product feel a little less premium on the wallet.
