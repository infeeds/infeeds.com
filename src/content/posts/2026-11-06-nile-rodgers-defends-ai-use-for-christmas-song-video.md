Nile Rodgers is standing firm on his decision to use artificial intelligence for his upcoming music video, despite facing significant pushback from fans and critics who question the creative merit of the approach.

The legendary producer and Chic co-founder recently unveiled snippets of an AI-generated video for a new version of "The Christmas Song," a 2020 charity collaboration with Rebecca Ferguson. What he intended as an innovative artistic endeavor quickly became a lightning rod for debate about technology's role in modern creativity.

## The Backlash Begins

When Rodgers first shared his "AI capture" on social media, the response was swift and negative. Commenters expressed disappointment, with one writing, "There's better way to preserve your legacy." The criticism centered on concerns about what AI has done to the broader artistic landscape, particularly in music.

Rodgers pushed back against the immediate judgment, asking people to "wait to you see it before you make judgement." But the backlash only intensified when he shared photo stills from the project days later. Multiple commenters renewed their objections, suggesting his platform was irresponsibly promoting controversial technology.

## The Defense

However, Rodgers didn't back down. Instead, he provided detailed explanations of his creative process. "We created everything," he told a commenter. "Mood boards, story boards and did it the same way we'd do a film. The AI did what we told it to do and we painstakingly created every second you'll see on the screen."

He also revealed practical reasoning behind the choice. The AI approach allowed him to produce a video for a project supporting ill children during the holidays without the $100,000 price tag a traditional film would require.

Rodgers didn't shy away from the broader philosophical debate either. In response to criticism about his use of AI while society grapples with its implications, he posed a pointed question: "It's somewhat hypocritical to say you're anti AI and you're using it all the time because it makes your life convenient."

## The Bigger Picture

The producer's stance on [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) technology extends beyond this single project. Earlier in 2024, he told Euronews about his nuanced view of AI in music. While he condemned using AI-generated vocals to imitate another artist's voice, he championed its use as a creative tool for artistic expression and musical innovation.

"I think that creativity comes from the artist," Rodgers explained. "Now, if you have a tool to help you become more creative, there's nothing wrong with that."

To illustrate his point, he drew historical parallels. When drum machines and synthesizers first emerged, skeptics dismissed them as "not real music." Auto-tune faced similar resistance before becoming integral to modern pop production. Rodgers frames AI as simply another tool in an artist's palette, no different in principle from these earlier technological innovations.

## Art Versus Technology

The controversy raises uncomfortable questions about art's future. Is AI merely a neutral tool, subject only to the intent of its operator? Or does its use fundamentally compromise the authenticity and soul of creative work?

Rodgers' approach to answering these questions centers on transparency and intention. He positioned this Christmas project not as a shortcut to artistic legitimacy, but as a means to serve a meaningful charitable purpose: bringing joy to hospitalized children during the holidays. The technology enables accessibility to artistic experiences that might otherwise remain out of reach for many creators.

Yet the fact remains that his use of AI has become the story, overshadowing his charitable impulse. Whether that's fair criticism or misguided technophobia likely depends on your perspective about innovation in [music](https://infeeds.com/tags/music) production.

The video arrives November 20. Only then will audiences judge whether Rodgers' faith in the technology was justified or whether his critics' concerns prove warranted.
