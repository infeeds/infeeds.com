Stray Kids just proved why they're one of the most dominant forces in music right now. Their latest mini album THIS & THAT secured a landslide victory in this week's Billboard listener poll, capturing 85% of the vote and leaving the competition in the dust.

The eight-track collection features the anthemic title track that listeners clearly can't get enough of. Released alongside the poll on Friday, the "This & That" music video has already racked up over 62 million views on YouTube, a testament to the group's massive global fanbase and their ability to create content that resonates instantly.

## A Stacked Week Gets Dominated

This wasn't just any ordinary week in [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment). Major artists dropped new material across multiple genres. Karol G released "No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto," Madonna collaborated with Kylie Minogue on the "Love Sensation" remix, and FLO, Role Model, and Alex Warren all competed for listener attention.

Yet when the polls closed on Sunday, Stray Kids emerged with overwhelming support. Their 85% victory margin wasn't just impressive; it was decisive. In a fragmented streaming landscape where preferences are usually spread thin, this kind of concentrated support speaks volumes about the octet's cultural moment.

The album opens with "RUN IT" before diving into the self-assured title track with that unforgettable "this & that, this & that" refrain. It's the kind of hook that burrows into your consciousness and stays there. The remaining tracks include "After You," "FARMING," "I Do," "Way Out," "Back Then," and a festival version of the title track, all released through JYP Entertainment and the REPUBLIC Collective.

## Chart Dominance Seems Inevitable

While it's too early to predict where THIS & THAT will land on the official Billboard 200 charts, Stray Kids' track record suggests expectations should be sky-high. Every single one of their previous entries on the Billboard 200 has debuted at number one. From ODDINARY in 2022 through DO IT in 2025, the group has maintained an undefeated streak that most artists can only dream about.

This consistency isn't luck. It's a combination of dedicated fanbase organization, strategic release planning, and genuinely compelling music that transcends the K-pop bubble and appeals to mainstream audiences worldwide. [Music](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) industry observers have been watching this trajectory closely, and the numbers keep supporting their dominance.

## What This Means for the Industry

Stray Kids' continued success raises interesting questions about the current state of music consumption. In an era where algorithmic playlists supposedly democratize discovery, these results suggest that passionate, organized fanbases still hold tremendous power. The group's ability to mobilize their supporters for both streaming and voting demonstrates a level of engagement that many legacy artists simply cannot match.

The music video's rapid accumulation of tens of millions of views also underscores how THIS & THAT was designed as a complete multimedia package. It's not just a song; it's a moment, a visual experience, and a cultural touchstone all rolled into one.

With another likely chart-topper on the horizon and this week's poll victory already in the books, Stray Kids continues to write their own success story in real-time.
