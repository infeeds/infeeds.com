September 29 brings more than just Goose Day and Poisoned Blackberries Day to celebrate. It's National Coffee Day, and unlike previous years, the major chains have largely abandoned their free-coffee giveaways in favor of new product launches. But here's the thing: the sales we're seeing on actual coffee equipment this year are genuinely impressive, potentially rivaling what you'd find during Black Friday.

Created by the National Coffee Association around 2009, National Coffee Day traditionally meant hitting up Dunkin' or Panera for a discounted cup. This year, Krispy Kreme is pushing coffee-flavored donuts and Starbucks is promoting their new aerocano instead. If you're chasing deals, your best bet is investing in equipment that'll serve you far longer than a single cup.
