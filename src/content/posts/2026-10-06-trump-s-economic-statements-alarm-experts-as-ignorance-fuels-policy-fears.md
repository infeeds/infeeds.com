## A Self-Proclaimed Genius Reveals Economic Blindness

Donald Trump recently claimed on Truth Social that the only guardrails artificial intelligence needs is "a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT," referring to himself. Yet within days, he made a series of statements so economically misguided that they prompted experts to question whether he poses a genuine threat to both the US and global economy.

Only 7% of Americans believe Trump should lead AI regulation, according to a recent Wall Street Journal poll. The advocacy group Republicans Against Trump went further, calling him the "Dumbest. President. Ever." His economic pronouncements have sparked particular alarm among economists and corporate leaders.

## The $1.5 Trillion Fantasy

Trump proposed eliminating trade with any nation showing a deficit with the US. He claimed this would generate "at least, 1.5 Trillion Dollars a year." The arithmetic here is, quite simply, absurd.

This reveals a childlike misunderstanding of how trade functions. If the US ended commerce with China, Mexico, Taiwan, and the European Union, manufacturers would face catastrophic shortages. Computer chips, rare earth elements, auto parts, aluminum, fertilizer for farmers, and lumber for construction would become scarce or inaccessible. Factories would close. Farms would fail. Construction would halt.

Trump views trade deficits as pure losses, seeing the word "deficit" as "nothing more than a fancy word for LOSS." Consider his logic applied to US-France trade. If Americans import $2 billion in wine while France imports $1 billion in Kentucky bourbon, Trump sees a $1 billion loss for America. In reality, Americans gain $1 billion worth of wine they wanted to purchase. That's a gain, not a loss.

## Interest Rates and Inflationary Delusions

Before the Federal Reserve raised rates to 3.75%-4% to combat inflation, Trump demanded they cut rates to 1% or half a percent. Any student who completed Econ 101 understands that extremely low rates fuel inflation. Yet Trump promised to reduce prices on day one while simultaneously demanding policies that would push them higher.

University of Michigan economist Justin Wolfers called this demand "the dumbest thing ever said by a president."

Then came the truly delusional claim: if the Fed cut rates as he demanded, the US could achieve GDP growth of "14, 15, 16 and 20 percent." These numbers are fantasies. The US economy grew at 2.2% in the second quarter of this year and rarely exceeds 5%. Since World War II, 20% growth occurred just once, in Q3 2020, when businesses rushed to reopen after pandemic lockdowns. Even that extraordinary bounce followed a historic 28% plunge.

## The Danger Is Real

These statements reveal a president increasingly detached from reality and basic economic principles. One must ask: where are his economic advisers? Why aren't they preventing him from making such dangerous pronouncements?

Trump has already turned absurd economic theories into policy. His trade war with Canada exemplifies how ideology becomes practice, harming ordinary Americans. [Recent trade tensions](https://infeeds.com/tags/news) continue to ripple through the economy, raising costs for consumers and businesses alike.

The larger concern involves what could happen if Trump implements these ideas more broadly. Tariffs and hostile trade policies have already hit working families' wallets. His conflict with Iran has contributed to economic chaos and uncertainty.

Republican Congress members either cheer him on or remain silent while Trump damages the economy and middle-class finances. Americans facing [economic pressures](https://infeeds.com/tags/economy) deserve leaders who understand basic economic principles.

Trump's lack of economic literacy isn't merely embarrassing. It's potentially catastrophic for millions of struggling families who can't afford further price increases, job losses, or economic instability.
