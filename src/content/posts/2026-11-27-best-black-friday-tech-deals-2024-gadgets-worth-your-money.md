Black Friday has officially arrived, and the floodgates are open. After weeks of staggered sales, we're now seeing the lowest prices of the year across nearly every tech category you can imagine. Whether you've been eyeing a new MacBook, waiting for AirPods to drop, or just want to upgrade your streaming subscriptions, now's genuinely the time to pull the trigger.

The deals this year look different than they have in the past. Thanks to price hikes across multiple categories, some discounts aren't quite as aggressive as previous years. But here's the thing: that doesn't mean they're not worth taking. Apple's latest MacBook Air finally hit its pre-price hike level for the first time since July. That's a deal.

## Audio and Wearables Worth Your Cash

If you're in the market for new audio gear, you've got solid options at every price point. Apple's AirPods Pro 3 deliver the best noise cancellation and sound quality in the entire AirPods lineup. They're more comfortable, more secure, and now include built-in heart rate tracking for workouts. That's genuinely useful stuff.

For headphone lovers, Sony's WH-1000XM4 noise-canceling headphones returned with minor improvements on what was already a great design. You get excellent active noise cancellation, that timeless foldable design, and performance that rivals Sony's newer flagships at a fraction of the cost. JBL's Tour One M3 is another solid pick if you want something with a touchscreen wireless transmitter and 70-hour battery life.

Nothing's Ear 3A wireless earbuds are worth mentioning too. They look striking, sound fantastic, boast active noise cancellation, and offer robust EQ settings. For the price, they're hard to beat.

## Tablets, Laptops, and Computing Power

Apple's new M5 MacBook Air is still expensive, but it's genuinely fast with significantly better performance than prior generations. The battery life and speakers remain excellent, making it worth the investment if you need a laptop for creative work or demanding tasks.

For something more affordable, Apple's MacBook Air A18 Pro is a solid option for students and everyday users. It has a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM. Meanwhile, Acer's answer to affordable ultrabooks features an Intel Core 5 processor and speedy Thunderbolt 4 ports.

On the tablet front, Apple's 11th-gen iPad gets the new A16 chip but remains the only Apple tablet without Apple Intelligence support. If you need an e-reader instead, the Kindle Paperwhite is a much better value than the base model, offering waterproofing, better battery life, and a larger 7-inch screen.

## Smart Home and Accessories

The SwitchBot Bot is one of those products that sounds gimmicky until you actually use it. Stick it on a standard button or switch and suddenly you can control your coffee maker, lights, pet feeder, and garage door from your phone. It's inexpensive and genuinely useful.

## Streaming Services Keep Getting Cheaper

You can grab an ad-supported Disney Plus and Hulu bundle for $4.99 a month for an entire year. That's eight dollars off and covers both streaming services with their deep catalogs. HBO Max Basic is offering similar value at $5.49 a month for a year, giving you access to shows like The Last of Us and films like Superman.

Peacock Premium is another solid option if you want NBC's vast content library, Universal Pictures films, and live sports all in one place.

## Gaming and Entertainment

The Nintendo Switch 2 is finally here with its 7.9-inch LCD display, magnetically attached Joy-Con controllers, and backward compatibility with most original Switch games. 007 First Light, meanwhile, is arguably the best action game released this year, featuring the kind of stealth and high-speed car chases you'd expect from a Bond game.

Sony's DualSense Wireless Controller for PS5 features immersive haptic feedback and resistance triggers that feel great to use. It works with PC, macOS, and mobile devices too.

## Power Banks and Charging Solutions

Anker's 25,000mAh power bank sports two built-in USB-C cables and tops out at 165W when charging multiple devices. Iniu's P55-E2 is more compact if you want something slim that can fully recharge an iPhone 16 twice over.

The deals won't last forever. While most will likely stick around through Cyber Monday based on prior years, there's no guarantee your specific item of interest will remain discounted. We've sorted through thousands to find the ones truly worth your money.
