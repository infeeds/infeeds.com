Cardi B is bringing her stadium-headlining debut to the Caribbean this October. BZR WKND Jamaica 2026 will take over Kingston's National Stadium from October 16 to 19, marking a major moment for the cross-genre festival and a significant milestone for the "Little Miss Drama" rapper.

The four-day celebration of music, culture, food, tourism, fashion and entertainment announced its complete lineup, with Grammy-nominated dancehall superstar Spice hosting the festivities. Beyond Cardi's headlining slot, the festival will feature R&B legend Keyshia Cole, emerging Caribbean talent Malie Donn, and a deep bench of dancehall performers including Ding Dong, Stalk Ashley, Shaneil Muir, and DJ MAC.

## A Major Moment for Keyshia Cole

Keyshia Cole's inclusion marks another significant career milestone. The "Love" singer mounted an arena-headlining tour across the U.S. and U.K. last year to celebrate the 20th anniversary of her landmark debut album, The Way It Is. Her BZR WKND performance follows her recent stadium debut during Usher and Chris Brown's smash R&B Tour, proving she's having a serious resurgence moment.

The timing couldn't be better. Her 2006 collaboration with Sean Paul, "(When You Gonna) Give It Up to Me," is experiencing renewed chart momentum, hitting number 3 on the Billboard Hot 100 back in the day and finding fresh audiences today. Cole will handle the R&B department while the rest of the lineup keeps the dancehall energy high.

## Dancehall's Rising and Established Stars

Malie Donn represents the next generation of Caribbean talent. Billboard named him its Caribbean Up-and-Comer for August, and he'll bring recent smashes like "Van Cleef" and tracks from his new Golden Child LP to Kingston. The energy will be undeniable when he takes the stage.

Shaneil Muir, nominated for female dancehall artist of the year at the 2026 Caribbean Music Awards, will showcase why she's one of the most exciting voices in the genre. Stalk Ashley, who collaborated with Billboard's R&B Rising Star Destin Conrad on the sultry track "Skin Out," will bring that same magnetic presence to [entertainment](https://infeeds.com/tags/entertainment) at BZR WKND.

Ding Dong didn't just show up to perform. The "Badman Forward Badman Pull Up" artist snagged dancehall performer of the year at the 2026 Caribbean Music Awards, promising a characteristically high-octane set. DJ MAC, who crafted the year's dominant Skippa-led "WYFL" riddim with CrashDummy, will keep the momentum rolling heading into Cardi's headlining moment.

## The Full Experience

The festival rounds out its roster with even more talent. Ricky Platinum won Male International DJ of the Year at the 2026 Caribbean Music Awards and will spin tracks. Plxyboy, Boom Boom, Zekedon, Shukkle Bus, Coppershot, Chromatic Live, Code Red, Karim Hype, Troopa Traloopa, Bloodline Franco, Fyah Man, MC Nuffy and Splakavelli complete the incredible lineup.

With "AH HA" still sitting at number 46 on the Hot 100 as of late September, Cardi B is riding strong momentum into her Caribbean stadium debut. The streets, as they say, are clearly ready for Bardi to hit that stage.

BZR WKND Jamaica 2026 represents something bigger than just another music festival. It's a celebration of Caribbean culture, tourism, and the undeniable influence dancehall and reggae have on global [music](https://infeeds.com/tags/music) culture. Ticket information is available through the official BZR WKND channels.
